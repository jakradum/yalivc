Swoosh editor

Drag the square anchors and the round handles. Filled anchors are sharp corners (the two tips); hollow ones are smooth, so their two handles stay in line. Double-click the green line to add an anchor. Shift-click an anchor to remove it. Alt-click an anchor to switch smooth / sharp. Arrow keys nudge the selected point (shift for bigger steps). Cmd+Z undoes. Save, and the main page rebuilds with your curve.
